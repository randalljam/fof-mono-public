### **Who gets what when you collect from a minion in co-op?**

**1\) The items (the physical drops)**

* The *items themselves* go to **whoever clicks/takes them** from the minion GUI (or whoever breaks/picks up the minion and grabs the contents). That part is basically “first hands on it \= in your inventory.”

**2\) Collection progress (recipe unlocks)**

* In co-op, **collections are shared** across the profile, and unlocking a collection recipe unlocks it for all co-op members. So if *you* collect cobble from a minion, that collection progress is effectively for **both of you**. 

**3\) Skill XP from minions (Mining/Farming/Foraging etc.)**

* Most minions give Skill XP when collected, and in co-op **when one member collects, the Skill XP is credited to each co-op member** (this was an update change). 

* Skills are still “separate” in the sense that you each have your own Mining/Farming level… but minion-collection XP can level both of you up because each of you receives it. 

* Community testing also suggests the *collector’s personal XP boosts* (god pot, pets, cookie, etc.) don’t necessarily apply to everyone else—others may get the “base” minion XP. (This part is community info, not an official rulebook.) 

**So why does your daughter see XP when you collect?**

Because co-op minion Skill XP is credited to **every co-op member** when any one of you collects.

## **A simple “fair co-op minion” ruleset that prevents toe-stepping**

Here’s a clean system that works really well for 2-player co-ops:

### **Rule 1: Split your slots into “Core” and “Project”**

With 8 slots, do this:

* **6 Core slots (shared, long-term):** stuff like Redstone/Coal/Lapis/Sugar Cane/Potato/etc.

* **2 Project slots (reserved):** **1 slot is yours**, **1 slot is hers**, used for “I need Cobble X” / “I’m pushing Carrot collection” style goals.

This instantly solves your cobblestone situation: **your Cobble minion lives in your Project slot until you hit Cobble X and craft what you need**.

### **Rule 2: Each minion has a “Steward”**

For every placed minion, pick one steward (put a sign behind it):

* **Only the steward upgrades it**

* **Only the steward collects it** *unless they explicitly say “go for it”*

This prevents accidental “I grabbed it because I was on the island” moments.

### **Rule 3: Every minion has an output chest policy**

Put two labeled chests near the minion area:

* **PROJECT chest** (for the steward’s active goal)

* **SHARED chest** (for communal crafting / selling)

Default policies:

* Project-slot minions → 100% to PROJECT chest

* Core minions → 100% to SHARED chest (or alternate days if you want it super fair)

### **Rule 4: “Goal locks” are respected**

When someone declares a goal, it’s locked until completion. Example sign:

**COBBLE PROJECT (RJ):** Don’t collect. Goal \= Cobblestone Collection X → craft Super Compactor 3000\.

Because collections are shared, she’ll still benefit from you doing it. 

But the *items* stay reserved so you can actually finish the craft.

### **Rule 5: Upgrade funding is explicit**

Pick one:

* **Steward pays** for upgrades on their stewarded minions, *or*

* **Shared bank pays** for Core minions; steward pays for Project minions

Either way—no ambiguity.
