# SkyBlock Island Invite Templates

Purpose: simple, friendly lines for inviting people to visit the island without sounding spammy, creepy, or like a rule-risky giveaway ad.

## Quick Safety Notes

- Do not advertise this as a "giveaway" in public lobbies. Hypixel's current server rules say giveaways and giveaway advertisements are not allowed.
- Do not spam the same message across hubs, lobbies, or instances.
- Do not post Discords, YouTube channels, social handles, links, or anything that looks like outside advertising.
- Do not ask people their age, school status, location, Discord, real name, or other personal info.
- Keep the kid/family context broad: "family-friendly co-op" is safer than "old man with young daughter."
- If someone makes it weird, pressures you, or asks for personal info, do not argue. Leave, block, or `/report` if needed.

## Best Default Wording

Use this kind of phrasing:

Hey! We just opened our island for friendly visitors. We have a small free starter chest with a few helpful extras. No payment, no trade, no links. `/visit rjcomp` if you want to stop by.

> Family-friendly co-op island open for friendly visitors. We have a small free starter chest with a few helpful extras. No payment, no trade, no links. `/visit rjcomp` if you want to stop by.

Why this works:

- It says "family-friendly" without giving your daughter's age.
- It says "starter chest" instead of "giveaway."
- It says "no payment, no trade, no links," which helps make the intent clear.
- It invites people to visit without asking them for personal details.

## Public Chat Templates

Use public chat rarely and keep it low-key. If Hypixel treats the free chest as a giveaway advertisement, public chat is the riskiest place to mention it.

Safer public line, no free-item pitch:

```text
/ac Family-friendly co-op island open for friendly visitors. `/visit rjcomp` if you want to stop by and say hi.
```

Slightly more direct, but more rule-sensitive:

```text
/ac Family-friendly co-op island open for friendly visitors. We have a small starter chest for newer players. No payment, no trade, no links. `/visit rjcomp`
```

Very short:

```text
/ac Chill family-friendly SkyBlock island open for visits: `/visit {my_username}`
```

## Guild Or Friends Chat Templates

These are better places to mention the starter chest because you are talking to people who already have some connection to you.

```text
/gc Our family co-op island is open if anyone wants to visit. We set up a small starter chest with useful extras. No payment or trade needed. `/visit {my_username}`
```

```text
/msg {name} Hey, thanks again for helping us early on. We set up a small pay-it-forward starter chest on our island if you ever know a newer player who could use a few basics.
```

```text
/msg {name} We finally made our island a little more visitor-friendly. Feel free to stop by sometime: `/visit {my_username}`
```

## Party Chat Templates

Use when you are already playing with someone and the invitation is natural.

```text
/pc After this, you're welcome to visit our co-op island. We have a little starter chest for newer players and some goofy family builds.
```

```text
/pc Thanks for running with us. If you want to visit our island later: `/visit {my_username}`
```

## Reply Templates

If someone asks what the chest is:

```text
Just a small pay-it-forward starter chest. People helped us when we were newer, so we wanted to keep a few useful extras for visitors. No payment or trade needed.
```

If someone asks whether they can take something:

```text
Yes, if it is in the visitor starter chest, take one or two things that are actually useful to you. Please leave some for other newer players.
```

If someone asks for expensive items or coins:

```text
Sorry, we are keeping it small and simple. The chest is just extra starter-help stuff, not custom requests.
```

If someone asks personal questions:

```text
We keep personal info private, but we are happy to talk SkyBlock. What are you working on in-game?
```

If someone is pushy:

```text
No worries, but we are going to keep it casual. Have a good one.
```

## Homeschool / Parent Wording

Avoid:

```text
Any young homeschoolers here? I am an old man playing with my young daughter.
```

Better:

```text
Any other family-friendly players or parent-kid co-ops around? We mostly keep chat clean and play casually.
```

Even safer:

```text
Any other family-friendly co-ops around? We mostly keep chat clean and play casually.
```

Best if you are in a guild or with trusted friends:

```text
Our co-op is a family project, so we keep things clean and low-pressure. Any other family-friendly players here?
```

## Island Sign Text

Sign near visitor chest:

```text
VISITOR STARTER CHEST
Take 1-2 useful items.
Please leave some for others.
No payment needed.
```

Sign near entrance:

```text
Welcome!
Family-friendly co-op island.
Please keep chat clean.
```

Sign near valuable/private storage:

```text
Private co-op storage.
Please do not ask for items here.
```

## My Rule Of Thumb

The safest version is:

```text
Family-friendly co-op island open for visits. `/visit {my_username}`
```

Mention the starter chest after someone arrives or in guild/friend/party chat. That keeps the public message from looking like a giveaway advertisement.
