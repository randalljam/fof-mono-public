## Rules & Game Approach

- **Game Mode**
  - Survival **only**.
  - No Creative, no flying, no spawning items.
- **Difficulty**
  - Normal survival play.
  - Very brief switches to **Peaceful** only when:
    - Helping 5-year-old **TreasureHunterM** with something stressful.
    - Pausing to figure out a mechanic in safety.
- **Cheats & Teleporting**
  - In a previous big survival world, the family **used /teleport and cheats a lot**.
  - In this world:
    - **No teleporting at all**, even when rjcomp (world host) plays alone.

- **Playstyle**
  - Family-focused world, shared progress.
  - Exploration, rail-building, and story are more important than pure efficiency.
- **Version and Mod**
  - World name is **"Lunter"**.
  - Minecraft version is **Java 1.21.10**.
  - Using mod **Biomes O'Plenty**; non-vanilla biomes, plants, blocks, etc. can appear in the story.

# Chapter 6 – The Sky-Trains of the Deep

Morning comes soft and golden to Salmon Coast. The sea is calm, the red cliffs glow in the light, and the rails between the Beach Camp and the Cave Base gleam like sleepy snakes. Today is not a day for wandering or wondering. Today, the Great Railway has a very clear to‑do list: finish the village line, fetch the magic books, and send llamas and happy ghasts down into the deep for goodies.

Down by the shoreline tracks, WildPetal and TreasureHunterM stand beside a gap in the rails—just a few missing pieces between the Beach Camp and distant Village Alpha. rjcomp checks the stacks of rails they carried back from the Mineshaft of Many Webs and nods.

“This is it,” he says. “Once we fill this gap, Village Alpha becomes a true station.”

They work together, placing the last rails along the sand. The line clicks into place, a shiny path hugging the edge of the water. When the final piece connects, there is a quiet, satisfying feeling—like the world itself saying yes. A test ride whisks them smoothly from Salmon Coast to the little village, and the villagers glance up from their work as the minecart rattles in.

“Welcome to Village Station A,” WildPetal declares proudly.

The celebration is short but happy. There is still work to do. Village Station A is not just a cozy stop; it hides the next step in their plan. Inside one of the cottages, a Librarian waits with books that shimmer faintly when turned toward the light. The pages whisper of Fire Aspect and Fortune—magic words that mean flaming swords and extra goodies from the stone.

TreasureHunterM helps with the trading, passing over emeralds and watching the precious books change hands. He hugs his Fire Aspect book to his chest like a treasure chest that learned to read. WildPetal chooses a Fortune book, already imagining shiny diamonds tumbling out of rock like candy.

“Books first,” rjcomp says, “then gear, then goodies.”

Back at the Cave Base, the Glazed Hall of Power hums softly. Its terracotta sigils curl and swirl like enchanted rail maps on the floor and walls. Today, it feels a bit like a tiny, colorful train office. rjcomp lays out the new books, iron armor pieces, and trusty tools.

One by one, they step up to the enchantment table. Letters float and swirl. A simple iron sword drinks in the Fire Aspect magic, ready to blaze when swung. A pickaxe learns Fortune’s secret, promising extra goodies from every rich vein it bites into. WildPetal and TreasureHunterM slip on their best armor—still iron, but now part of an official “crew kit.”

“We look like real railway guardians now,” TreasureHunterM says, turning in place to make his armor clink.

“Good,” rjcomp replies. “Because our next helpers float.”

Out in the canyon between the south cave and the north cave, the air is full of drifting light. WildPetal’s happy ghasts bob gently over the river, round and glowing like friendly balloons with faces. Below them, her two llamas munch calmly on sparse grass near the rail bridge, tails flicking.

“This is where we build the harnesses,” WildPetal decides.

Together they start sorting rope, leads, and little signal flags. rjcomp helps measure safe distances and tie secure knots. TreasureHunterM tests how far a ghast can drift while still staying near the group.

They don’t want chains; they want gentle tethers, like reins on very floaty ponies. Soon, a few happy ghasts wear soft harness loops that glow faintly when tugged, and the llamas sport tidy saddlebags for snacks, torches, and spare tools.

“That one is Torch Ghast,” WildPetal says, pointing to a ghast with extra lanterns clipped to its harness. “And that one is Lift Ghast, for helping us go up and down.”

TreasureHunterM points to another. “That’s Scout Ghast. It peeks around corners so we don’t bonk our faces.”

The llamas get names too, of course: Snack Llama and Spare-Pickaxe Llama, ready to wait at the cave entrance with supplies. The plan is simple but exciting: the llamas will guard the surface “station,” while the ghasts become the first-ever sky-trains of the deep, drifting through the caves with the crew.

In the story of the day, Torch Ghast will hover ahead, gently lighting new tunnels like a moving lantern. Scout Ghast will float near ledges, peering down and warning the crew about drops. Lift Ghast will help mark safe up-and-down paths in the deepest shafts. And everywhere the ghasts go, the crew will follow, chipping goodies out of walls and floors until the north and south caves are scraped almost bare.

When preparations are done, the canyon grows quiet. Rails hum. The river murmurs. Llamas snort softly. Happy ghasts bob at the ends of their glowing tethers, ready to drift wherever WildPetal leads.

At the mouth of the south cave, rjcomp gathers everyone together.

“Today,” he says, “we’re not just miners. We’re the Armored Crew of the Deep. Our job is to clear these caves of goodies and make them safe for future rails. WildPetal leads the sky-trains. TreasureHunterM, you’re Cart Captain and Goodies Collector. I’ll watch the front. If PumaJockey joins us, she’ll be our master ore-finder.”

TreasureHunterM nods, clutching his new flaming sword. WildPetal holds Torch Ghast’s tether with one hand and a stack of torches in the other, eyes bright. The ghasts glow like little moons. The llamas shift their weight, saddlebags full and ready.

The cave mouth waits, cool and dark, like the open door of a long-forgotten tunnel station.
