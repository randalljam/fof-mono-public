**_Single-Digit Addition Problem Categorization_**

This is a transcription of the color-coded addition-problem categorization from the image.

**Counting note:** the main categories below count each commutative problem once as "canonical", using the orientation `smaller addend + larger addend`. That gives **55 canonical problems** from 0–9, with the "expanded" set totaling 100 problems, which includes the complements (sometimes called "turn-around" or "reversed"). The final “Hardest Six” are a **subset of the Tough 21**, not extra problems, even though they are useful to name separately for practice.


## Quick Summary
| Category | Canonical Count | Expanded Count | Pattern |
|---|---:|---|
| Add Zero | 10 | 19 | `0 + n` |
| Add One | 9 | 17 | `1 + n`, starting at `1 + 1` |
| Add Two | 8 | 15 | `2 + n`, starting at `2 + 2` |
| Doubles | 7 | 7 | `n + n`, from `3 + 3` through `9 + 9` |
| Tough 21 | 21 | 30 | Remaining non-double problems with addends 3–9 |
| Hardest Six | 6 | 12 | Subset of Tough 21: both addends are 6 or higher, but not doubles |
| Total | 55 | 100 | Excludes double counting of Hardest Six
