How this works — read once, then it's muscle memory

  • 1. Get paper and pencil ready. You'll solve this by hand, showing all work.
  • 2. Hit record, then think out loud the ENTIRE time. This feels weird at first — do it anyway. Say every thought: what the problem is asking, what you're writing, why, and every doubt. If you go silent for a while, the AI sees that too.
  • 3. Stuck or confused? Say so out loud ("I don't remember how to find b") — naming the confusion is exactly what gets you a targeted lesson. You can also hit “I'm stuck — teach me” for an immediate mini-lesson.
  • 4. When done, stop the recording and photograph your written work. Then submit both.

Example of thinking aloud: "Okay, it's asking for the equation of a line through two points. First I'll write the points down… slope is change in y over change in x, so sixteen minus seven over five minus two… that's nine over three, so three. Now I plug in the point two-seven to get b… seven equals six plus b, so b is one. Let me check with the other point: three times five plus one is sixteen — yes, that matches."
